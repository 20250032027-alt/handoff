## 1. Getting a video onto your phone

**iPhone (Safari)**
1. Tap the video link in the table above.
2. Tap **View raw** (or the **⋯** menu → **Download**). The video opens full screen.
3. Tap the **Share** button → **Save Video**. It lands in Photos.
   - If you don't see Save Video: Share → **Save to Files**, then open Files, long-press the video → Share → Save Video.

**Android (Chrome)**
1. Tap the video link in the table above.
2. Tap **⋯** → **Download** (or **View raw**, then the ⋮ menu → Download).
3. It saves to *Downloads*. Instagram, TikTok and YouTube can all pick it from there.

Do the same for the **cover** image if you want a custom cover.

> Tip: copy a caption *before* you open the app. Every caption below is in a box with a **copy** button in its top-right corner.

## 2. Posting checklist (every platform)

- [ ] Upload the video **as is**. It is already 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, with the music baked in. Don't add music from the app's library (it would fight the beat, and the video is timed to its own track).
- [ ] Keep the app's **original audio** at 100 % volume.
- [ ] **Don't add text stickers or captions in the app**: the video already has its captions, and app text covers the subject.
- [ ] Pick the cover: on Instagram use **Add from camera roll** with the cover jpg; on TikTok choose the first second (the title frame).
- [ ] Paste the caption, then post.
- [ ] Right after posting, drop the **pinned comment** and pin it.

## Facts used (so you can answer comments)

- **GAU-8/A:** 7 barrels, 30 mm, fixed rate of 3,900 rounds/min (= 65 per second), barrels spun by two hydraulic motors, drum holds up to 1,174 rounds.
- **T-72:** 125 mm gun, carousel autoloader under the turret holding 22 two-piece rounds (projectile + propellant charge). The gun moves to a loading position, a hoist lifts the cassette, the rammer seats the projectile then the charge, and the spent stub is ejected through a small port in the turret roof. Practical reload is roughly 7–8 seconds.
